How the numbers are made. Prop rpm is an estimate for a loaded flat-plate prop of 129 mm: tip speed about 0.65 × wind for the flat 45° blades, about 1.0 × wind for the twisted set. The worm gives one stroke per 24 turns. The clutch lets go when wind passes 14 − 2 × gap m/s and catches again once it drops 2 m/s below that. All figures are ± 50 %.
